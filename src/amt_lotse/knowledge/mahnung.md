title: Mahnung / Zahlungserinnerung (payment reminder)
triggers: Mahnung, Zahlungserinnerung, Mahngebühr, gemahnt, Erinnerung

---
A Mahnung or Zahlungserinnerung is a reminder that a payment is late. It can come from a company or from an authority, and it may add a fee (Mahngebühr). A Mahnung is different from a gerichtlicher Mahnbescheid: that one comes from a court (Amtsgericht / Mahngericht), has its own short deadline written on it and needs a fast reaction.

Tip: If the reader already paid, they should check the payment date and the reference on the transfer.

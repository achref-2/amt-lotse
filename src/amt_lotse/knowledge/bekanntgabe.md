When a letter says a deadline runs "after Bekanntgabe" (or after Zustellung), the deadline does NOT start on the date printed on the letter. It starts when the letter legally counts as delivered: for a decision sent by post this is a few days after it was posted, by law. So never say "within one month of the letter's date". Say "within one month after you received the letter (Bekanntgabe)". Do not calculate an exact end date for the reader.

Tip: Write down the day the letter arrived; if the exact end of the period matters, ask an advice centre.

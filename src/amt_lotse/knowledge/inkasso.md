An Inkassounternehmen is a PRIVATE debt collection company that a creditor pays to collect money. It is not an authority, not a court and not a bailiff. It has no special rights: it may not seize belongings or enter a home; only a court bailiff (Gerichtsvollzieher) may do that, and only after a court decision. Inkasso usually adds extra costs. Translate it as "debt collection company", never as "enforcement agency".

Tip: Paying a correct bill before it goes to Inkasso avoids the extra costs.
Tip: If a demand from Inkasso looks wrong, the Verbraucherzentrale can check it.

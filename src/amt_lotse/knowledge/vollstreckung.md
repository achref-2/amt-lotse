Vollstreckung means the state collects money by force: for example a court bailiff (Gerichtsvollzieher), or an authority such as the tax office collecting its own claims. It can include taking money from a bank account (Pfändung). This is serious and only possible with a legal basis. Only use the word enforcement if the letter itself talks about Vollstreckung, Pfändung or a Gerichtsvollzieher.

Tip: If a letter announces Vollstreckung, react before the date named; a debt advice centre (Schuldnerberatung) helps for free.

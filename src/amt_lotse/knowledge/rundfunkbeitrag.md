The Rundfunkbeitrag is a fee for public radio and TV, paid per apartment, not per person, whether or not you own a TV or radio. In a shared flat only one person pays; the others tell the Beitragsservice the name and contribution number (Beitragsnummer) of the person who pays.

Tip: Letters from the Beitragsservice should always be answered, otherwise reminders follow.
Tip: People receiving certain social benefits or BAföG can apply to be exempted.

An invitation (Einladung) from the Jobcenter to an appointment is binding. Missing it without an important reason can reduce the payments; the letter says what applies, so do not state percentages or amounts of a reduction unless the letter states them. Since July 2026 the payment Bürgergeld is called Grundsicherungsgeld; letters may still use the old word, it means the same payment.

Tip: If the reader cannot come, they should tell the Jobcenter BEFORE the appointment and give an important reason, for example a doctor's certificate of inability to attend.

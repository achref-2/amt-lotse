A Beitragsrückstand means health insurance contributions (Beiträge) have not been paid. Health insurance is compulsory in Germany.

Tip: If the reader cannot pay the full amount, they should contact the Krankenkasse soon and ask about paying in parts (Ratenzahlung).
Tip: If they already paid, they should keep the proof of payment.

The Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung is the standard paragraph at the end of an official decision. It explains how the reader can object (Einspruch or Widerspruch), where, and within which period. It is information about a right, not an instruction: the reader only needs to act if they disagree with the decision. It does not replace or postpone anything else the letter asks for, such as a payment.

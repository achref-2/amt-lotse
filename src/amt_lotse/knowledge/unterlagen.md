When an office asks for documents (Unterlagen, Nachweise), the reader has to send them by the date given. Only describe consequences of not sending them if the letter itself names them; do not guess consequences such as delays, penalties, further letters or advice.

Tip: Write the reference number on every document.
Tip: If the documents cannot be sent in time, ask in writing for more time BEFORE the deadline ends.

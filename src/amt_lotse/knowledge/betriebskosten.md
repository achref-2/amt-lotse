A Betriebskostenabrechnung (also Nebenkosten- or Heizkostenabrechnung) is the landlord's yearly statement of costs such as heating, water and rubbish. The result is either a Nachzahlung (the tenant pays the difference) or a Guthaben (the tenant gets money back). A change of the monthly advance payment (Vorauszahlung) in the same letter is a separate announcement for the future; it does not depend on whether the tenant pays the Nachzahlung. Einwände / Einwendungen are written objections against the statement.

Tip: Tenants may ask to see the receipts; a tenants' association (Mieterverein) can check the statement.

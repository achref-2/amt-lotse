title: Widerspruch (objection against Jobcenter, Krankenkasse, Familienkasse, city offices)
triggers: Widerspruch, Widerspruch einlegen, Widerspruchsfrist
sources: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_2/__39.html https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgg/__86a.html
---
A Widerspruch is a formal written objection against a decision (Bescheid) from an authority such as the Jobcenter, Krankenkasse, Familienkasse or a city office. It must be sent within the period named in the Rechtsbehelfsbelehrung. Whether a Widerspruch stops the decision for the time being depends on the kind of decision; for many Jobcenter decisions (for example reductions or cancellations of payments) it does not. So never promise that a Widerspruch stops a payment, a cut or a demand.

Tip: Before objecting, an advice centre (Beratungsstelle) can check the decision for free.

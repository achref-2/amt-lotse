title: Rückmeldung / Exmatrikulation (university re-registration)
triggers: Rückmeldung, Semesterbeitrag, Exmatrikulation, exmatrikuliert
sources: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__16b.html
---
The Rückmeldung is the re-registration for the next semester; it usually happens by paying the semester fee (Semesterbeitrag) on time. Exmatrikulation means being removed from the university's list of students. That ends the student status, which can also affect health insurance, the student ticket and, for international students, the residence permit.

title: Guthaben / Erstattung (money the reader gets)
triggers: Guthaben, Erstattung, erstatten, zurückerstattet, überzahlt, Rückzahlung an Sie, zu Ihren Gunsten
sources: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__556.html
---
A Guthaben or Erstattung is money the reader RECEIVES, for example after paying too much. It is not a bill: the reader does not have to pay it. Sometimes the office needs the reader's bank details (IBAN) to send the money; then that is the only thing to do.

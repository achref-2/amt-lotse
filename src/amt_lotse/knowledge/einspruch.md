An Einspruch is a formal written objection against a decision (Bescheid), mostly from the tax office (Finanzamt). It is free. Important: an Einspruch does NOT stop the payment. The amount in the decision must still be paid by the payment date, unless the tax office agrees in writing to pause it (Aussetzung der Vollziehung, which must be applied for separately). Paying does not mean giving up the objection. Never write that objecting means the reader does not have to pay.

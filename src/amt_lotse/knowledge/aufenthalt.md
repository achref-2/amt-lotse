An Aufenthaltstitel (for example an Aufenthaltserlaubnis) is the residence permit. To extend it, the reader must apply BEFORE it expires. If they apply in time, the old permit usually continues to count until the Ausländerbehörde decides, and they can get a Fiktionsbescheinigung as proof. Applying late can cause serious problems, so the date in the letter is very important.

Tip: A migration advice centre (Migrationsberatung) helps with residence questions for free.
